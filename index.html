HOW TO PLAY

Keep the rhythm

Alternate AD or ←→ like little feet. About 9 presses a second is the sweet spot. Press faster and the wheel overheats. Hit the same foot twice and the hamster trips.

Jump the pencils

SPACE jumps. Pencils lie across both lanes. A row of chilis across both lanes is also a jump.

Switch lanes

WS or ↑↓. Toilet rolls are too tall to jump, so go around them. Grab golden seeds for a burst of speed. Skip the chilis, they heat you up.

Freeze for the cat

A cat peeks in, then opens its eyes. While it's looking, don't run. One step and your hamster jumps out of its fur. An autoclicker can't tell the difference.

Catch a Block Drop

Only during a live run: when the real hamster completes a Squeekchain block mid-race, a cyan block lands ahead of both of you. Roll through it for a boost.

On a phone: tap the left and right foot pads, with buttons for jump and lane.

FAIR PLAY, NO BOTS

The server is the referee

Your browser only sends key presses. The server replays your whole race from them using the same engine, so a modified page can't fake speed or a finish.

A course nobody picks

Before the race the server locks in a hidden seed and shows you its hash. Both players add their own random seed. After the finish the seed is revealed and your browser checks the course was really built from it.

The road shows up late

Obstacles are sent only a few seconds before they reach the leader, so nothing can read the whole course in advance.

Rhythm signature

People wobble and scripts don't. A press rhythm that's too perfect is flagged and kept off the leaderboard.
